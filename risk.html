Risk Disclosure

Trading and investing involve financial risk. This disclosure describes important risks users should understand before making investment or trading decisions.

Last updated: August 2026

Important Risk Warning

The value of financial assets can rise or fall. Trading and investing may result in partial or total loss of capital. No user should commit funds they cannot afford to lose.

No Guaranteed Investment Returns

Wfunded's current website is a prototype. Investment figures, projected amounts, account balances, performance percentages, market prices and trading activity currently displayed are illustrative interface examples. They are not guarantees of profit, verified historical performance, live account results or live market quotations.

01

General Investment Risk

All financial investments involve some degree of risk. Market conditions can change rapidly and investment values may fluctuate significantly.

Investors may receive less than the amount originally invested and, depending on the product, may lose their entire investment.

02

Market Volatility

Financial markets can experience rapid price movements caused by economic events, political developments, interest rates, company announcements, market sentiment, liquidity conditions and other factors.

Prices may move significantly within very short periods of time.

03

Loss of Capital

Trading or investing can result in loss of some or all committed capital. Users should carefully consider their financial situation, objectives and ability to tolerate losses.

Money needed for essential living expenses, emergencies or short-term obligations should not be exposed to inappropriate investment risk.
04

Cryptocurrency Risk

Cryptocurrency markets may be particularly volatile and can involve risks different from traditional financial markets.

  • Extreme price volatility.
  • Changing regulatory requirements.
  • Blockchain or network disruptions.
  • Cybersecurity threats.
  • Liquidity limitations.
  • Custody and private-key risks.
  • Technology or protocol failures.
05

Stock Market Risk

Shares may lose value because of company performance, economic conditions, industry developments, management decisions, investor sentiment or broader market events.

A company's past performance does not guarantee future results.

06

Foreign Exchange Risk

Foreign exchange markets can move quickly due to interest-rate changes, monetary policy, economic data, geopolitical developments and changes in market liquidity.

Currency movements may also affect the value of investments denominated in currencies different from a user's home currency.

07

Commodity Risk

Commodity prices may be affected by supply and demand, weather, geopolitical events, production levels, transportation conditions, currency movements and global economic developments.

08

Liquidity Risk

Some assets may become difficult to buy or sell at the desired price. Limited liquidity can cause larger price movements, wider spreads or delays in executing transactions.

09

Leverage & Margin Risk

If Wfunded ever offers leveraged or margin-based products, those products may amplify both profits and losses.

Small market movements can produce disproportionately large changes in account value when leverage is used.

Wfunded should not enable leveraged trading in production until the product structure, eligibility requirements, risk controls and applicable regulatory obligations have been established.
10

Technology Risk

Online financial services depend on software, internet connectivity, infrastructure and third-party systems. Technical problems may interrupt access to accounts or services.

Potential issues include network failures, software errors, maintenance, cybersecurity incidents, data-provider outages and external service disruptions.

11

Cybersecurity Risk

Financial accounts may be targeted by phishing, credential theft, malware, impersonation or other cyber threats.

Users should protect login credentials and authentication codes and should verify that communications claiming to come from Wfunded are legitimate.

12

Third-Party Risk

A live platform may depend on third parties for market data, payments, custody, identity verification, infrastructure or other services.

Failures, interruptions or financial problems affecting those providers may affect platform services.

13

Regulatory & Legal Risk

Laws and regulations governing financial assets can change. Regulatory developments may affect product availability, trading, custody, taxation or the ability to access certain markets.

14

Market Data Risk

Prices, charts and market information may be delayed, inaccurate, unavailable or supplied by external providers.

Market values currently displayed on the Wfunded prototype are demonstration data. They must not be described as live until a legitimate market-data service is connected.
15

Hypothetical & Illustrated Performance

Hypothetical examples do not represent actual trading results and cannot account for every factor affecting real-world performance.

Actual outcomes can differ substantially from mathematical examples, projections or simulations.

The 3-day, 5-day and 10-day investment examples currently shown in the Wfunded prototype are illustrative scenarios only. The displayed percentage figures must not be interpreted as promised or guaranteed profits.
16

Past Performance

Historical performance, where legitimately reported, is not a reliable guarantee of future performance.

Users should not make investment decisions solely because an asset or strategy previously generated positive returns.

17

Tax Risk

Trading and investment activity may create tax obligations. Tax treatment can vary by asset, transaction type and jurisdiction.

Users may need independent tax advice regarding their individual circumstances.

18

No Personalized Financial Advice

General market information displayed by Wfunded should not automatically be treated as personalized financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

Users remain responsible for evaluating whether a financial product or decision is appropriate for their circumstances unless a properly authorized advisory service is explicitly being provided.

19

Risk Management

Users should understand the products they use and consider appropriate risk-management practices.

  • Understand potential losses before committing capital.
  • Avoid concentrating all capital in one asset.
  • Consider liquidity needs.
  • Review fees and product terms.
  • Protect account credentials.
  • Do not rely on guaranteed-profit claims.
20

Final Risk Statement

Before using any future live Wfunded financial service, users should carefully review the relevant product documentation, fees, risks and eligibility requirements.

Wfunded's final production risk disclosures will need to reflect the actual products offered and the laws of the jurisdictions in which those products are made available.
