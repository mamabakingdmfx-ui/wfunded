Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I review my account?

Sign in and open My Account to review your profile and recorded funding activity.

How do I submit payment information?

Select an investment option and continue to the payment page, where the transaction information can be submitted for verification.

What information should I provide for payment support?

Include the transaction hash, network, amount and your account email whenever those details are relevant.

Will Wfunded ask for my password?

Do not send passwords, private keys, recovery phrases or authentication codes through the support form or by email.
